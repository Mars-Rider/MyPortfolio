## Instagram Posts

Individual Instagram posts and reels can be embedded right on this page — paste the post link on its own line as `[embed](https://www.instagram.com/p/POST_ID/)` and it renders as a normal Instagram embed. (A full profile feed isn't something Instagram allows embedding directly, so this only works one post at a time.)

Example — swap this for a real post link from your account and it'll render inline:

```
[embed](https://www.instagram.com/p/POST_ID/)
```
