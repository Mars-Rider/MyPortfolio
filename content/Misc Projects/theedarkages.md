# Thee Dark Ages

In *Thee Dark Ages*, you play as a knight tasked with protecting a farmer from incoming bandits! However, keeping him safe won't be easy—the bandits are equipped with bows, and the terrified farmer runs away from *you*!

A small game built completely from scratch using Unity.

---

### Controls

* **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** – Move
* **Spacebar** – Quickly play, pause, resume, or restart
* **Escape** – Pause game
